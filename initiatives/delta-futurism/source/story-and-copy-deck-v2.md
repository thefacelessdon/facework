## 1. The story in one paragraph

The Delta has shaped how the world works, moves and makes culture. Delta Futurism starts from that inheritance and asks what can be made next: places to live, room to build, new ways to learn, and a culture that keeps moving. Some of the region's people carried its music and ideas far away; others stayed and built schools, businesses and neighborhoods. Today the next chapter already has people in it, and real change is arriving alongside them. The future the page imagines is organized around daily life, and it lasts when the people here hold a real share of it. The work is practical: research, storytelling, design and cultural planning with the people and institutions already building the region. Arkansas Cypress is the first place to try it.
