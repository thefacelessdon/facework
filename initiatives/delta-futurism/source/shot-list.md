The page has three kinds of images that real photos should replace. In priority order:

| Priority | What | Count | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Empty frames marked "Still to photograph" | 4 | The page argues the next chapter "has people in it already." These are the proof, and right now they're blank. |
| **2** | Present-day photos whose rights aren't cleared | 5 | They can't be shared publicly as they are. Reshoot or license them. |
| **3** | AI concept images in the future section, "What it makes possible" and the proposal | 25 | They're the part that looks most like AI. Replace them with real **seeds**: places where a small version of the idea already exists in the Delta. |

A shoot day that covers Priority 1 and 2 makes the page shareable. Priority 3 can come in rounds.

## How the photos should look (all priorities)

- **Look:** documentary color, eye level, natural light. People doing what they actually do, not looking at the camera. The page's archive photos are Dorothea Lange, Marion Post Wolcott and Russell Lee; aim for the same kind of looking in 2026.
- **People with agency.** Show people with each other and with their tools and work, not as anonymous labor or as background figures.
- **Avoid:** posed portraits, a stock-style laptop classroom, festival or stage imagery, nostalgic or "retro" styling, decay or abandoned buildings, branded signage as the subject.
- **A good phone is acceptable** if the light is good. Shoot RAW or the highest-quality JPEG available.
- **Format:** landscape unless noted. The page crops to **4:3**. At least **1600 px wide** (the cover needs 2400). Shoot one vertical frame of each as well.

## Priority 1 · The four empty frames

### 1.1 · Cover: a lived-in main street
- **Where:** Downtown Pine Bluff, a block with at least one open business: Main Street or a nearby downtown block.
- **When:** a weekday late morning or a Saturday, when people are actually out.
- **In frame:** storefronts, sidewalk, two to six people going about their day (walking, talking, going into a shop, sitting). Some sky and building above street level.
- **Avoid:** an empty street, boarded-up storefronts as the focus, events or festivals.
- **Format:** crops to about **2:1**, so leave room above and below. At least 2400 px wide.
- **Replaces:** the 2020 mural stand-in (`pb-mural-2020.jpg`). That photo is public domain, so it can stay as a fallback.

### 1.2 · A musician at work
- **Where:** a real Delta space where music gets made: a rehearsal room, home studio, church music room or venue soundcheck. Pine Bluff first, then anywhere in the Arkansas or Mississippi Delta.
- **In frame:** the process, not a performance pose. Hands on an instrument or gear, a producer at a laptop and keyboard, a band working through a part. Show the room around them.
- **Who:** a working musician or producer making new music, not only blues heritage. The page's line is "new work from old ground."
- **Fills:** "Still to photograph: A musician at work" (Present · "The next chapter has people in it already").

### 1.3 · A teacher and her students
- **Where:** a classroom, after-school program, library, workshop or community class: anywhere people are learning something together.
- **In frame:** a teacher and two or more students working on something real: building, writing, measuring, playing, reading together. Learning between generations is welcome.
- **Avoid:** a row of students on laptops.
- **Minors:** see Permissions. Without releases, frame children from behind or with faces out of focus.
- **Fills:** "Still to photograph: A teacher and her students."

### 1.4 · A business opening its doors
- **Where:** a locally owned Pine Bluff business at opening time: a café, barbershop, grocery, hardware store, kitchen or shop.
- **In frame:** the owner or staff unlocking, turning the sign, setting up the counter, the first customer coming in. The shopfront or the inside of the room should be readable.
- **Also shoot:** one wider frame that shows the street.
- **Fills:** "Still to photograph: A business opening its doors."

## Priority 3 · Concept images → real seeds

**The idea.** A concept image shows a future that doesn't exist yet, so a camera can't replace it directly. What a camera *can* show is the seed: a place in the Delta where a small version of the idea is already happening. A real seed photo is stronger evidence than an AI picture, and it fits the page's argument that the next chapter "has people in it already."

**Decided 2026-11-18 (Stedmon): "Already here."** A real seed photo replaces the concept image in the same slot. What changes and what stays:
- **Label:** "Already here" replaces "Concept image · imagined, not planned."
- **Caption:** a real one: who (if they agreed to be named), what they're doing, place, month and year, and the photographer's credit.
- **Kept:** the latent-to-color effect and the ownership line, so the photo still comes into full color at the line that says what stays with the people here.
- **Mixed is fine.** Slots without a seed photo keep their concept image and label until one is shot. A section can hold both.
- **Rejected:** moving the seeds into the Present section and keeping the AI images in the future. That would leave the AI images in the section that carries the page's argument.

## One shoot day: route by place

Grouped so one or two days covers Priority 1 and 2 and the easiest Priority 3 seeds.

1. **Downtown Pine Bluff, late morning:** 1.1 cover, 1.4 business opening, main street (`f-mainstreet`), mural (`f3-mural`). If it's market day, add the market (`f3-market`).
2. **Port and riverfront:** the river (P2), riverfront path (`f3-greenway`). Port crew (`f-river`) only if permission was arranged.
3. **Sherrill and the county roads, afternoon light:** the road (P2), the field from a public road (P2), a farm family (`f-field`) if arranged.
4. **A school, library or UAPB (book ahead):** 1.3 teacher and students, library (`f3-library`), mixed-age table (`f3-studio`), media (`f3-media-lab`).
5. **Separate bookings:** 1.2 musician (a rehearsal), Saturday night (`f-saturday`), a public meeting (P2), a kitchen prepping a big order (`f3-kitchen`).

## Permissions (required before anything is published)

1. **Model release** from every identifiable adult, saying the photo may be used on the Delta Futurism website and in its print and social materials.
2. **Parent or guardian release** for every identifiable minor, plus the school's or program's sign-off.
3. **Property permission** for any interior or private property (studio, classroom, shop, port working area, farm).
4. **No endorsement implied.** Tell each person plainly that being photographed does not make them a partner in, or supporter of, Delta Futurism or the Community Prosperity Partnership. The caption will say only who and where they are, if they agree.
5. **Photographer's license:** written permission to use the photos on the website and in Delta Futurism and The Current materials, with a credit line. Agree on any fee in writing first.
6. **Solar sites:** shoot only from public roads. Nothing inside a project fence, and nothing that identifies a family's leased parcel.

## Delivery

- **File names:** `DF-<slot>-01.jpg`, using the slot names above. Examples: `DF-cover-01.jpg`, `DF-teacher-01.jpg`, `DF-river-01.jpg`, `DF-seed-kitchen-01.jpg`. Number the alternates 02, 03 and so on.
- **Send:** 3–5 selects per shot at full resolution, plus the signed releases.
- **For each select, include caption notes:** who (name and role, only if they agreed to be named), what they're doing, place (business or venue name, town), month and year, and the photographer's name for the credit line.

  Example: *"Owner Jane Doe opens her café on Main Street, Pine Bluff, December 2026. Photo: Photographer Name."*
