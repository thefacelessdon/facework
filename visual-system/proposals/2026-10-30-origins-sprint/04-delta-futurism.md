# 04 — Carrying Delta Futurism ("same system, two bodies")

**Recommendation in one line:** Delta Futurism is a **domain and an issue series
inside the Facework system**, not a sub-brand with its own look. It gets its own
tag, its own issue accent, and its own nameplates. It never gets its own palette,
type, or mark.

## Recommendations

1. **Domain tag, not a brand.** `DOMAIN · DELTA` on channels, findings, and
   issues (02 §2). That keeps Delta Futurism inside the open-domain rule: Delta is
   one domain among unnamed future ones, not a fourth fixed pillar.
2. **Same type, same paper, same mark.** Literata / Schibsted / Spline Sans Mono
   and the Coherence Mark carry Delta surfaces unchanged. "Seen through Facework's
   visual language" means exactly this.
3. **Its own issue accent, chosen from the place.** A Delta issue series picks its
   per-issue accent (01-brief §2) from the region's real material: river silt,
   cotton, Delta red clay, juke-joint paint. Recorded in the colophon with its
   source. Covers only, under the same rules.
4. **Maps are the Structure / Lineage instrument.** City pods, the AR–MS–LA
   corridor, and cultural-infrastructure networks render as Facework diagrams
   (FVI-300 symbol grammar, hairline, shape law), not as tourism maps or
   infographics. Pods still forming are squares; established ones are circles.
5. **Archive-first for history.** Regional history (blues lineage, Civil War
   sites, the Underground Railroad threads flagged as gaps in CONTINUITY) enters as
   cited archive blocks with sources. Apply the dignity rules (01-brief §6) without
   exception.
6. **Avoid the regional clichés.** No Southern-gothic texture, no sepia-wash
   "authenticity," no blues-font nostalgia. Stead's Oct 28 turns question the
   authenticity of the blues narrative. The system should let a finding *test*
   that narrative, not illustrate it.
7. **Voice.** The Faceless Don can frame Delta work as a byline and prologue, as
   with any domain. Local voices are quoted and credited as themselves. The
   narrator never speaks for the region.
