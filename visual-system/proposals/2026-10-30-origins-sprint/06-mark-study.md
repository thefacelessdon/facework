# 06: Mark study (mark and motion, derived together)

> **Current call (2026-10-30): M2**, per [07-trademark-check.md](07-trademark-check.md). M1 failed the lookalike check (Reg. 3630396) and is recast as the unregistered attention box. Drawing pass and attention-box spec: [08-m2-drawing-and-attention-box.md](08-m2-drawing-and-attention-box.md); files in `marks/m2/`.

Stead (2026-10-30): the current mark hasn't earned "keep" yet; the mark is open
to reinterpretation; coherence decides, not imitation of the Times.

So this study doesn't start from a shape. It starts from **what the mark has to
do**, derived from canon and the field notes, and scores candidates against
that. Each candidate is specified together with its motion, the way the Times
specified the 175 numerals and their motion under one brief (FN-01).

## 2. Candidates

### M1: The Frame (recommended)

**Form:** four square-capped corner strokes, drawn with the wordmark's stroke
(8 units on its 80-unit cap height), around an empty **4:5 portrait** space.
The corners never meet; the middle of each side stays open.

**Derivation:**
- **Four corners = the four constructs** (Signal, Frequency, Current,
  Stability). **The empty interior = Coherence:** the open center, now literally
  the whole field. This is the same meaning FVI-001 already governs, said more
  simply.
- **The open sides = Entropy/exchange:** FVI-001 reads Entropy as the system
  meeting the world. The frame never closes; the world passes through it.
- **A portrait frame with no face** = facelessness (C5) and the narrator as a
  lens. The 4:5 ratio is the portrait ratio, and the social format the
  references use (the NYT cutdown is 3:4, UPLOAD and Prada are portrait).
- **Perception completes it:** the eye closes the gaps into a frame. That's the
  Origins thesis (identity is perceived and completed by others) built into the
  form, without a face.
- **"Facework"** is also Goffman's term for the work people do to maintain
  "face" in interaction ("On Face-Work", 1955; cited from memory, not
  verified this session). A frame around an absent face *is* face-work.

**Motion (designed with the form):** the mark **is** the attention box (05 P3).

1. In use, its corners cut (hard cut, no tween) from one piece of evidence to
   the next, framing what's being checked, like the Times' orange boxes.
2. When it stops on nothing, an empty frame, it *is* the logo. The end card is
   the frame landing on empty space beside the wordmark, cut on, held (FN-01
   end card).
3. Scale shifts by cutting, never tweening. At UI size the strokes thin to 1px
   verdigris; at mark size they're ink at full weight.

So one object does two jobs: the system's verification behavior and its
identity. That's "mark and motion designed together" taken to its end point.

**Risks:**
- Corner brackets are a common UI motif (camera focus, scanners) → C8.
  Mitigations: the fixed 4:5 proportion, the wordmark-weight square-capped
  stroke, the open middles, and the fixed lockup. **This is the main risk and
  needs a real distinctiveness check** (a trademark and lookalike search) before
  adoption.
- It loses the recovered bilateral history of the current mark (FVI-001
  "Recovered source"). That's lineage Stead may value.

### M2: The Coherence Mark, redrawn monoline (evolve)

**Form:** the current four-strand bilateral mark, redrawn as wordmark-weight
monoline strokes instead of filled wedges, open center kept.

- Keeps lineage and meaning (C1).
- Gains family resemblance with the wordmark (C3).
- Still reads "butterfly" first (FVI-001 admits this, "a butterfly only at first
  glance"). Not faceless-as-lens; just not a face.
- **Motion:** draw-in of the strands (existing FVI-400). That's a logo
  animation, not a system behavior (fails C4).

### M3: Register monogram "FW" (minimal)

**Form:** the wordmark's own F and W, cut out as a monogram.

- Perfect family resemblance (C3) and complete on arrival (C2).
- Carries no canon meaning (fails C1). Generic.
- **Motion:** none intrinsic.

## 4. Recommendation

**M1, The Frame**, conditional on a distinctiveness check. It's the only
candidate that meets the criteria derived from both the canon (open center, four
constructs) and the research (complete on arrival, family resemblance, motion
as a working behavior). It also changes the system less than it seems: it keeps
the open-center clause, the four-construct meaning and the wordmark, and turns
the already proposed attention box into the identity itself.

**If the distinctiveness check fails**, fall back to M2. It keeps the lineage
and fixes the family-resemblance gap.

## 7. Issue numerals (the 175 logic)

*From FN-01:* the 175 numerals echo the Fraktur h, k and s, "a family
resemblance, not a logo placed next to a logo."

Every Facework digit is built only from strokes already in FACEWORK:

| Digit | Built from |
|---|---|
| 0 | the O, narrowed |
| 1 | the K stem and arm angle |
| 2 | the R bowl, the A leg and the E base bar |
| 3 | the R bowl, doubled |
| 4 | the A diagonal and the E crossbar |
| 5 | the E top bar and stem, plus the R bowl |
| 6 / 9 | the C opening, the O and the R bowl |
| 7 | the E top bar and the A diagonal |
| 8 | two R bowls |

The issue lockup sets mark, wordmark and number on **one baseline at one cap
height**, as the Times sets "The New York Times 175" (FN-01, full film, about
1:11).

## 8. Motion study (M1), on the board

Four scenes, looping, scripted in `board.html`:

1. **Accretion:** six archive blocks cut on one at a time (about 300 ms each),
   and the holdings line counts up. *FN-03 calendar; FN-01 #MeToo quotes,
   0:12–0:15; 05 P4.*
2. **Attention box:** the M1 frame, at UI scale (2px verdigris), cuts between
   cited blocks (about 380 ms each, no tween). *FN-01 verification squares,
   0:38–0:42; 05 P3.*
3. **State eases:** the checked block's marker eases from open square to
   settled circle (520 ms, `--rr-ease-settle`), and the holdings update.
   *05 P2, the second clock.*
4. **Lockup:** the issue lockup cuts on complete and holds about 2.8 s.
   *FN-01 end card; 05 P1.*

With reduced motion, or without scripts, the settled channel shows, static.
Verified in headless Chrome with `--force-prefers-reduced-motion`. The Claude
preview pane doesn't run inline scripts, so the sequence there was checked by
running its code by hand.

## 9. Recommendation

> **Superseded by [07-trademark-check.md](07-trademark-check.md) (2026-10-30).** A live US registration (Reg. 3630396, class 35, branding services) is nearly identical to M1 alone, and the motif is generic (Unicode U+26F6, Google's `crop_free` icon). **Revised call: M2 is the identity mark; The Frame stays as the unregistered attention box.** The original reasoning is kept below for the record.

**Take M1, The Frame, forward.**
- It's the only option where **the mark and its motion are one object**: the
  verification box the system already uses (FN-01) is the identity, and the end
  card is just that box landing on empty space beside the wordmark (FN-01 end
  card). That's the strongest reading of "build the mark and its motion
  together."
- It keeps the canon meaning (FVI-001: four constructs, open center).
- It shares the wordmark's stroke, and its numerals follow the 175 logic
  (FN-01).
- It's faceless by construction (handoff; FN-02 warns against characters).

**Condition:** a trademark and lookalike check on corner-bracket marks before
any drawing pass. **Fallback:** M2.
