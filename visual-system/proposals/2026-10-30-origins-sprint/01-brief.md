# 01: Visual-system brief

**Stance, from the Times (FN-01):** one thing holds absolutely still, the
evidence moves around it, and a single thin accent points at what's being read.
That's the whole system in one sentence, and the Reading Room already has the
pieces: the Coherence Mark holds, the Index moves, verdigris points.

Every rule below cites the field note it comes from. Rules without field-note
support are marked **held** (kept from canon, no new evidence) or
**unsupported** (dropped or waiting on a source).

Masthead is **ruled** (Stead, 2026-10-30): "A public record of coherence" stays.
"The study of digital identity, autonomy, and perception" is the Index's
field-of-study line beneath it.

## 4. Mark rules

1. **Locked identity unchanged:** Coherence Mark (open center) and Register
   wordmark. *Held.*
2. **The mark holds still.** In any composition with moving content, the
   lockup stays put. In the full Times film the 175 lockup **cuts on, complete,
   and holds** (about 1:11–1:13). Proposed: Facework's draw-in (FVI-400) is kept
   only for the identity moment; everywhere else the lockup cuts on whole
   (05 P1, open question). *FN-01 full film.*
3. **Numerals and nameplates join the family by sharing strokes,** not by
   placing a second logo beside the first. *FN-01 §3/§8.*
4. **Mark and motion are designed together.** The next design job is a
   Coherence Mark motion study, before any nameplate. *FN-01 §4 (via Brand
   Identity), 05 "Open."* Blocked on the NYE launch film for reference.
5. **Faceless Don has no mark.** *Held.* FN-02 shows the risk: charming
   characters take over a campaign.

## 6. Dignity rules (identity research)

Kept from revision 1. FN-01 adds one sharp point: the Times can collage the
Pentagon Papers and "Portraits of Grief" because **that archive is its own
reporting.** Facework shows other people's history only as **cited** archive
blocks: source, context and caption. Never as texture, collage filler or cover
art. Real people's archival images are never animated, warped or treated
(05 P5).
