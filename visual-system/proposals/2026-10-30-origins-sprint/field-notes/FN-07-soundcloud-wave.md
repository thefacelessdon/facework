# FN-07: SoundCloud wave

**Headline finding:** the useful part is the **SoundCloud player's grammar**:
comments pinned at moments on the waveform, and played vs unplayed shown by
color. The orange particle wave on black from the mood board has **no
traceable source.** I found no original, only generic audio-reactive particle
demos.

## 1. What I opened (via research agent)

- **Comments on the waveform:** SoundCloud added waveform comments to its mobile
  app on 2019-04-26 (secondary source: https://www.hiresaudio.online/?p=2400).
  Timed comments and a Waveform.js library were announced on the developer blog
  around 2012; I only have search snippets, as the pages returned 404:
  https://developers.soundcloud.com/blog/category/announcements/page/4
- **Visual spec (unofficial, third-party, unverified):**
  https://www.webdesignhot.com/design.md/soundcloud/
  - a mirrored waveform about 70px tall;
  - played portion in solid orange #FF5500, unplayed in grey #999999;
  - a 1px orange playhead;
  - 16×16 avatars pinned at comment timestamps.
- **Brand colors:** #FF5500 plus black and white (brand-kit site, secondary):
  https://brandkit.pro/brand/soundcloud
- **Waveform as data:** the track API's waveform JSON (a samples array) is drawn
  as bars: https://www.freecodecamp.org/news/how-to-make-realtime-soundcloud-waveforms-in-react-native-4df0f4c6b3cc/
- **Particle refs:**
  - Phonon, an 800-particle audio-reactive orb with configurable colors:
    https://github.com/nehasriva/phonon
  - Codrops 2023 tutorial on Three.js audio particles (403; snippet only):
    https://tympanus.net/codrops/2023/12/19/creating-audio-reactive-visuals-with-dynamic-particles-in-three-js/
- **Not found:** the specific image on the mood board, or any X post Stead saved.

## 8. What it means for Facework

- **Annotations pinned to a point on a timeline** is the strongest idea here. For
  the Index: a long source (audio, film, a long read) shown as a horizontal trace,
  with margin notes and findings **pinned at their exact moment.** That's the
  archive block's note, placed in time. It's also how these field notes cite
  frames.
- **Played vs unplayed = read vs unread.** A trace can show progress honestly,
  in ink vs muted, never in an accent color.
- **Drop:** the particle cloud and the glow. Nothing sources them, and DESIGN.md
  bans glow. Keep only the trace as a Field/Trace instrument (05-motion P7).
- **Avoid:** #FF5500. It's SoundCloud's brand and it collides with Facework's
  exposure and attention status hues.
