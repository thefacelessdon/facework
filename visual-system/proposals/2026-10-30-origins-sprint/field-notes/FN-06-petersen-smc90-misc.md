# FN-06: Petersen, smc90, img-6204

## A. Robbie Petersen, "Generative Networks" (strategy, not a look)

**What I opened:** a public mirror of the X post
(api.fxtwitter.com/status/2115430174439170243; the direct x.com fetch returned
402). The article is at x.com/i/article/2115308954008076288, posted 2026-10-28.
The author's bio lists him as an a16z investment partner. I read the article
**summary from the mirror, not the full article.** No Substack copy was found.

**Thesis, as summarized:**
- Past networks each grew around one media unit, capped by how many people could
  make it.
- AI makes creation cheap. On generative networks every output can be remixed
  into a new input, so networks grow by *creations*, not creators.
- The winning tool becomes the place where generated work lives and gets remixed
  (citing Dixon 2015, "come for the tool, stay for the network").

**Color:** the article's cover is about 95% one color, RGB(211, 251, 30), an
acid green. That's the second acid green in the source set (UPLOAD label
#CCE302, FN-03). The mood board probably took its green from both.

**What it means for Facework:**
- Every archive block needs a **stable, citable ID** so it can be remixed and
  still trace back. Remix without lineage is exactly the mediated identity
  Facework argues against.
- **"Come for the tool, stay for the network"** is a strategy question for Stead
  (is the Index a tool people use, or a publication they read?), not a visual
  one. Flagged, not designed.
- **Avoid:** the zine-collage look on the mood-board panel. Nothing in the
  article supports it; it was a generated summary.
