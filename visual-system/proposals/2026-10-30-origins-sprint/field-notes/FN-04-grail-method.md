## 8. What it means for Facework (from the method, not from the film)

- **One book, and rereading is the method.** This is a strong argument that the
  Index should have a **"reread" surface**: a view that brings older blocks back
  up in order, not just a "latest" feed. Review is where meaning comes from.
- **No to-dos in the creative book.** The Index holds material and notes, never
  tasks or status chrome. (This matches DESIGN.md's ban on dashboard patterns.)
- **Full notebooks go in a box.** A finished volume is closed and kept whole. For
  Facework: a channel can be **closed as a volume** (issued, circle marker), and
  a new volume starts. Closed volumes are browsable, never edited.
- **Scraps tucked between pages** maps onto the archive block: a captured thing
  plus a short note about why it's there.
- **Not yet supported:** any visual rule (page texture, handwriting, spreads).
  The mood-board panel's "aged notebook spread" was a generated image, and I
  have no frame from the real video to check it against. So the first-pass
  "notebook ground" texture rule is **unsupported until the video is seen.**
