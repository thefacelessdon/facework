# 09: Trademark lookalike check, M2 (corrected)

## Verdict

**M2 is clear to keep drawing. No strong lookalike was found.** Four live
records are moderate on *structure only*. I viewed every drawing next to the
corrected M2:

- None combines M2's specific construction: **monoline curved strands, an
  open center, and the lowest strand rising in an arc to cross the third.**
- **Mark + FACEWORK together: weak at most, against every hit.** No hit carries a
  similar word element.

**But protection would be thin.** Symmetric line-wings and butterflies are a
crowded, template-grade motif:
- 77 butterfly icons across about 39 open icon sets
- Noun Project "Butterfly-Line" and "Wings-Line" categories
- marketplaces selling ready-made line-butterfly logos

So M2's distinctiveness comes from the exact drawing, and above all from **the
composite with FACEWORK**, not from the motif.

**Call:**
- **Proceed with M2.**
- **File (if ever) as the composite** (`marks/m2/m2-filing-composite.svg`),
  not the mark alone.
- **Keep the rising crossing.** It's the feature that separates M2 from every
  close hit. Don't simplify it away.
- **Watch** the Full Send Aerotech application (99920113, class 35, not yet
  examined).

> **Ask a trademark attorney before registering anything.** This is a designer's
> lookalike screen, not a clearance opinion. Open for counsel:
> - an eye check of the 82 marks coded butterfly + curved lines and the 184
>   coded wings + curved lines (US);
> - WIPO/EUIPO (Vienna 03.13.01 butterflies, 26.11 lines);
> - common-law use;
> - the "Facework" name (07: Kyle McDonald's 2020 game).

## 3. Genericness

- Iconify: 77 "butterfly" and 26 "wings" icons across about 39 open sets
  (Tabler, MDI, Phosphor, Lucide Lab): https://api.iconify.design/search?query=butterfly&limit=999
- Noun Project "Butterfly-Line" category: https://thenounproject.com/browse/icons/term/butterfly-line/
- Template marketplaces (seen in search results; the site is behind a bot
  check): https://scalebranding.com/product/407549 , https://scalebranding.com/product/412923
- Zarla's guide recommends "abstract wings, made up of different-sized lines":
  https://www.zarla.com/guides/wings-logo-ideas
- DesignCrowd, 43 butterfly logos: https://blog.designcrowd.com.au/article/1736/43-butterfly-logos-to-make-your-brand-fly
- Adobe Stock line-butterfly icon: https://stock.adobe.com/images/this-vector-icon-illustrates-a-butterfly-with-a-clean-and-minimalistic-black-line-design-perfect-for-use-in-web-projects-or-graphic-designs/980303242

**Reading:** the "line wings" idea can't be owned. M2's specific drawing,
especially with the wordmark, is what's protectable. This is the same lesson as
07, and M2 survives it because its distinguishing feature (the rising crossing)
isn't in any template or hit found.
