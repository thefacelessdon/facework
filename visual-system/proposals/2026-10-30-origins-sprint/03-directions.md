# 03: Three directions

All three keep the locked identity and the ratified masthead (Stead,
2026-10-30). They differ in which source leads.

## A. The Record Holds (Reading Room, extended). Leads with FN-01

- **What it is:** paper and ink, the lockup fixed on a clear band, the Index's
  evidence moving around it on hard cuts, verdigris as the single pointer (the
  attention box).
- **Sources:**
  - FN-01: still mark, moving evidence, one pointing accent, real artifacts
    shown flat.
  - FN-05: tight rule set.
  - FN-07: notes pinned to a trace.
- **Strong:** the only direction whose core idea is *seen in a primary source*
  (48 sampled frames). No locked clause is touched.
- **Weak:** it needs real archive material to work. With an empty Index, it's
  just a quiet page.

## B. Work Beside the Work (Process Layer). Leads with FN-02

- **What it is:** every finished reading has a linked process layer of real
  roughs, notes, slates and construction lines, published next to it, never
  blended into it.
- **Sources:**
  - FN-02: finished film plus separately published pencil stills; slate labels;
    peg holes; red construction lines rhyming with the product.
  - FN-04: one book, reread (secondhand).
- **Strong:** makes "visible process" literal and honest.
- **Weak:** depends on Facework actually keeping its process material. The
  notebook look itself is unsupported (FN-04, video not watched).

## C. The Drop (Issue Register). Leads with FN-03

- **What it is:** numbered issues with one loud accent label each, halftone for
  reproduced images, channel lineups that build as networks.
- **Sources:**
  - FN-03: UPLOAD's single green tab on grey halftone; the calendar's lineup as a
    growing network.
  - FN-06: remixable blocks with stable IDs.
- **Strong:** built for X and the Are.na → X pipeline. Accretion is a strong
  motion idea.
- **Weak:** the accent still amends DESIGN.md §4. The research found nothing on
  The Upload as a series (FN-00), so the "issue" model rests on one still.

## Recommendation: A, with B and C as layers (unchanged, now evidenced)

**A is the base.** It's the strongest-evidenced direction and it fits the
ratified masthead: a *public record* is exactly "the record holds, the evidence
moves."

- **B** rides along as the linked process layer.
- **C** contributes one accent label per issue, plus accretion as channel motion.

**What changed from revision 1:** the case for A is no longer "least conflict
with canon." It's that A is the idea most directly seen in the primary source.
And C's footprint shrank from a full accent cover to a single label, because
that's what the real UPLOAD still shows.
