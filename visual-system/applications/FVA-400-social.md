# FVA-400 — Social

Social artifacts are complete propositions with visible provenance—not quote
cards and not substitutes for the source. The system supports three jobs:

- **Statement / 1:1:** one claim and one source path;
- **Evidence / 4:5:** observation, evidence, limitation, and destination;
- **Sequence / 16:9:** a relationship or transformation across frames.

Platform UI safe zones are treated as boundaries. Type remains readable without
zoom, URLs are ordinary text, and every export ships with a caption and concise
alt text. Semantic accent appears only when the post communicates that state.
