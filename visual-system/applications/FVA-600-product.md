# FVA-600 — Product surfaces

Product identity lives in visible state, consequence, and lineage. The reference
is a Lineage Inspector for a canonical artifact. It privileges the object and
its relationships over dashboard metrics, exposes upstream and downstream
dependencies, and lets a practitioner switch between map and structured list.

The desktop rail becomes an in-flow orientation header on small screens. A
bilateral relation becomes an ordered upstream/current/downstream sequence.
Actions remain 44 pixels high, focus is visible, selected state is redundant,
and no animation is required to understand a change.

Reference: `product/reference/index.html`.

Facework Field is documented separately in `FVA-610-facework-field.md`. It is an
experimental reflective experience, not a governing product pattern or a
replacement for the Lineage Inspector reference.
