# FVA-500 — Environments

Environmental identity performs recognition, orientation, interpretation, or
threshold—not decoration. A three-distance hierarchy governs every installation:
identity at approach distance, destination at decision distance, and evidence at
reading distance. Arrows and destinations outrank brand presence during movement.

Materials should expose construction and age honestly. Tactile and Braille
requirements, mounting height, glare, contrast, sightline, fire code, and local
accessibility law are production gates. Reference drawings are design intent,
not fabrication-ready shop drawings.
