# FVA-300 — Presentations

A Facework deck is a sequence of controlled fields. Orientation, proposition,
structure, evidence, and implication unfold over time; slides do not imitate
document pages. Quiet Paper frames alternate with Field thresholds. One
dominant idea governs each frame, while status and source remain visible.

The static export must preserve the argument. Type is sized for a room, diagrams
carry explanatory notes, and motion is limited to progressive explanation or
Exchange Resolve. Speaker notes contain sources and the intended spoken bridge.
