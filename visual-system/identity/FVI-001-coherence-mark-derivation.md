# FVI-001 — Coherence Mark Derivation

## Decision

**Axis Exchange** is selected as the canonical Facework Coherence Mark at
version 0.1.0.

The mark retains the inherited bilateral structure while reducing the historic
artifact from five visually competing strands to four semantic layers — the four
constructs Coherence holds in relationship (Cultural Physics §VII). It is a
butterfly only at first glance. Its governing reading is two fields maintaining
relationship through an open center under tension.

**The mark is the governing equation drawn.** Coherence = (Flow × Resonance) /
(1 + Entropy). The four strands sweep toward the center — Flow read in the
sweep, Resonance in the rhythm of the layers. The lowest strand crosses under
tension: Entropy, the friction held rather than erased. And the center stays
**open** — Coherence is the relationship the equation resolves to, never a term
placed between the others and never a filled node. The resting form therefore
has **no node at all**; open-center is the only logo form.

This decision establishes a design direction and production asset. It is not a
legal trademark clearance or a guarantee of registrability.

## Recovered source

The historic mark did not exist as a source asset in the Facework repository. It
was recovered from the transparency mask embedded on page one of the source PDF
`FACEWORK: THE COHERENCE OPERATING SYSTEM`.

The recovered artifact contains:

- three paired upper strands;
- two crossing lower strands;
- a hard vertical discontinuity at center;
- bilateral reflection;
- no body, antennae, enclosure, or literal biological detail.

That absence matters. The mark already behaved more like a field diagram than an
illustrated butterfly.

## Meaning

### The four layers

The four core strands are the four constructs Coherence holds in relationship,
defined canonically in [The Theory of Cultural
Physics](../../theories/cultural-physics.md) §VII, "The four constructs of
Coherence." Each strand is one construct (plain-language gloss in parentheses):

1. **Signal** (Truth) — what the system is.
2. **Frequency** (Expression) — how the system becomes perceptible.
3. **Current** (Behavior) — what the system repeatedly does.
4. **Stability** (Infrastructure) — what allows the system to hold under pressure.

The strands are not separate departments or a fixed top-to-bottom hierarchy.
They are the distinct constructs whose alignment determines whether identity can
carry weight over time. The mark does not introduce a new model; it renders the
one the Theory already governs.

Read against the governing equation, the same four strands carry its dynamic
terms: **Flow** is the sweep of the strands toward the center, **Resonance** is
the rhythm across the layers, and (below) **Entropy** is the crossing held under
tension. The strands are the constructs; the equation is how they behave.

### The bilateral field

The two sides do not have permanent labels. They may express internal/external,
identity/environment, theory/practice, or signal/field depending on application.
Their constitutional meaning is **counterforce in relationship**.

Assigning permanent labels would turn a transferable relationship into a narrow
diagram.

### The open center (Coherence)

The center is intentionally unfilled, and stays unfilled in every form. It is
**Coherence** itself — the term the governing equation resolves to, the
*synchronization* of the others, not a thing placed between them. Coherence is
not an element set between the forces; it is the maintained relationship that
keeps them in intelligible alignment. **It is never a filled node.** The resting
mark carries no center dot, square, or circle: the relationship, not a point, is
the meaning.

The interval also prevents the mark from becoming a literal insect body, neural
column, or closed institutional seal.

### The exchange (Entropy)

The lowest layer changes direction toward the center. This is **Entropy** in the
equation — the friction the system holds under tension rather than erases. It is
the visible tension that prevents symmetry from becoming static, and it
represents feedback, transformation, and the fact that infrastructure must adapt
when a system meets the world.

## Differentiation review

The review considered three crowded identity territories:

1. **Consumer butterfly** — multicolor, freedom, aspiration, personalization,
   and transformation. Microsoft's launch description for the MSN butterfly
   explicitly attached uniqueness, aspiration, freedom, and personal empowerment
   to the symbol.
2. **Literal butterfly company** — organizations whose name and logo use the
   butterfly directly, including Butterfly Network's current brand assets.
3. **Neural / AI abstraction** — symmetrical wings, brains, nodes, infinity
   loops, gradients, and central glowing bodies.

Axis Exchange separates itself through:

- monochrome structural bands instead of biological wings;
- an open rather than embodied center;
- four declared layers instead of decorative segmentation;
- one controlled directional exchange rather than a generic infinity loop;
- a document and systems context rather than transformation or AI spectacle.

Remaining risk: at small or context-free sizes the mark will still be read as a
butterfly. This is accepted as a secondary reading, not the governing derivation.

## Scale system

### Core mark

Use at 32 px high and above. The four layers, central interval, and lower exchange
must remain distinct.

### Micro mark

Use from 16 through 31 px. The micro form reduces four layers to three and uses
angular rather than curved construction. It preserves axis, bilateral balance,
and exchange while removing detail that cannot survive.

The micro mark is an optical implementation, not a different symbol.

## Color

The mark inherits the current foreground color. Canonical base expressions are:

- Ink on Paper;
- Signal Light on Field;
- one semantic state color only when the artifact itself is communicating that
  state.

Do not use gradients, independent wing colors, transparency effects, outlines,
or multiple semantic accents inside the mark.

## Clear space

Minimum clear space is one central interval on every side of the mark. Increase
space in identity and document contexts. The mark may participate in a diagram
only when the diagram does not make the identity asset appear to be a data node.

## Use rules

Do:

- use the supplied vector asset;
- preserve aspect ratio and central interval;
- use the micro asset below the core minimum;
- keep the mark monochrome;
- treat butterfly recognition as a secondary reading.

Do not:

- add a body, antennae, enclosure, or literal wing texture;
- rotate or skew the mark;
- animate the completed mark as flapping wings;
- assign different colors to layers without an analytic purpose;
- use either half independently as a generic wing;
- reconstruct the core mark from the micro asset.

## Motion principle

Animate the forces that form the mark, not the butterfly.

A future canonical motion study may begin with four distributed layers, reveal
the axis, introduce counterforce, and settle into Axis Exchange. The resting mark
must remain still. FVS-800 governs timing and reduced-motion behavior.
