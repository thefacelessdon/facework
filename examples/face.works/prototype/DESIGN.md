# face.works Production Design Contract

Status: validated implementation candidate
Governing source: `../../../visual-system/`

## Purpose

The public runtime is an interface to a living discipline. It helps readers
move from current observations toward models, frameworks, evidence, experiments,
lineage, and the practice behind the work.

It is not a protocol microsite, SaaS dashboard, agency portfolio, or exposed
repository.

## Audience

Practitioners, architects, implementers, runtime developers, maintainers,
collaborators, and prospective clients need to understand how Facework thinks,
inspect its evidence, and inherit useful artifacts without depending on the
original creator.

## Character

Calm, exacting, and alive. The physical reference is a durable public standard
crossed with a strategist's working notebook. Authority comes from visible
relationships, state, and lineage—not decoration.

## Public architecture

1. Field Notes
2. Models
3. Frameworks
4. Cases
5. Conversations
6. Experiments
7. Library
8. About

Legacy `/protocol`, `/proof`, `/status`, and `/engage` routes remain as deep
reads and operational records. They no longer govern top-level navigation.

## Visual roles

- Axis Exchange and the Register lockup identify the practice.
- Public Sans carries reading and display typography.
- Berkeley Mono or JetBrains Mono carries identifiers, state, and metadata.
- Paper mode carries reading and comparison.
- Field mode is reserved for thresholds and explicitly entered experiences.
- Clarity blue communicates active orientation and canonical state.
- Rules, open intervals, and sequences replace repeated rounded card grids.

## Prohibitions

- no decorative pattern field in the public shell;
- no mono-only reading experience;
- no neon-on-black “technical” costume;
- no generic rounded card grids;
- no butterfly-shaped decoration;
- no stale token or artboard source may override FVS;
- no experimental pattern may be presented as diagnosis, biometric identity,
  or canonical mark.
