## Design Context

Derived via the 14th & Co derivation method (epistemology → governing metaphor →
color/type/layout/mark/motion laws → instrument library → taste contract),
applied to Facework's own meaning. Not a reskin of 14th & Co — a sibling in the
same "operating register" family with its own metaphor, accent, and instruments.

### Governing metaphor (keystone)

**Facework doesn't decorate — it reads.** face.works is a reading room /
observatory. Every surface is a *reading* of a system's coherence — what it
carries, whether it holds under pressure, what survives its author — never a
portfolio or a brochure. Where 14th & Co *instruments transfer* (operational),
Facework *instruments attention* (diagnostic/perceptual).

### Users

Two audiences, two modes:
- **The Work** — readers of the published thinking + research (creators, cultural
  operators, strategists, studios). Job: read a reading; follow the lineage.
- **The Practice** — prospects evaluating whether/how to commission Facework
  (creators, cultural brands, studios). Job: see the proof, understand the
  method, engage.

### Registers (two states of one system)

- **The Record** — light, warm-paper, *settled/issued readings* = **The Work**.
- **The Field** — dark, live, *under observation* = the Lorenz Field + **The
  Practice** working surfaces. Publishing a reading = crossing Field → Record.

### Brand personality

Calm, exacting, alive. Observational and diagnostic. Structural honesty over
performance. "A public record of attention." No agency theater, no technical
costume.

### Aesthetic direction

Sibling to 14th & Co's operating register; own governing metaphor. Monochrome-
first; **color only classifies** (status palette reserved), one brand accent —
**Verdigris** (oklch ~0.55 0.075 190; coherence that holds and ages), ink-black
CTAs. OKLCH, warm neutrals tinted to Facework's own hue (distinct paper from 14th
& Co's sail). Type (locked): **Literata** (reading/body), **Schibsted Grotesk**
(structure/display), **Spline Sans Mono** (record voice) — none reflex, none
shared with 14th & Co. Keep the Coherence Mark: the governing equation drawn —
four strands sweeping to an **open center** (coherence is the relationship, never
a filled node), the lowest crossing under tension (entropy). Open-center is the
only logo form. The lockup leads with the mark, then the custom **Register**
wordmark (real letterforms, not a font). Full spec in `DESIGN.md`.

### Instrument library (derived from Cultural Physics, not copied)

Perceptual/diagnostic instruments where 14th & Co's are operational:
- **The Reading** — Signal: what a system truly carries, stripped of performance.
- **The Field / Trace** — Frequency/Current: the bounded pattern over time (the
  Lorenz field already embodies this).
- **The Structure / Lineage** — Stability: what holds, what survives handoff.
- **The Pressure reading** — Entropy: where coherence is failing.
- **The Coherence verdict** — the synchronization state.
Every visual is one of these (or argues its way in). No decoration.

### Design principles

1. Every surface is a reading, not a page.
2. Meaning precedes expression — nothing is designed until it can be derived.
3. Color only classifies; authority comes from exact relationships, not ornament.
4. Two registers of one system; the Record is settled, the Field is live.
5. The mark is the equation drawn; its center is always open — coherence is the relationship, never a filled node.
6. Transmission outranks performance; calm is controlled energy.
