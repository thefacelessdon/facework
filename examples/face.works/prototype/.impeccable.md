## Design Context

### Users

Facework serves practitioners, architects, implementers, runtime developers,
maintainers, collaborators, and prospective clients. They need to understand
the discipline, inspect its evidence and lineage, and inherit artifacts without
depending on the original creator or tool.

### Brand personality

Facework is calm, exacting, and alive. It communicates structural clarity,
continuity, confidence, and human intelligence without agency theater or
technical costume.

### Aesthetic direction

A durable public standard crossed with a strategist's working notebook:
editorial-institutional, engineered without sterility, minimal with meaning.
Paper is the primary reading mode; field mode is reserved for thresholds and
explicitly entered experiments.

### Design principles

1. Meaning precedes expression.
2. Architecture and lineage remain discoverable.
3. Authority comes from exact relationships, not ornament.
4. Transmission outranks performance.
5. Calm is controlled energy, not visual inertia.
6. Identity lives in behavior.
