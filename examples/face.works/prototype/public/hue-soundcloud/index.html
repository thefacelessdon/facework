The Long Run.

HUE × SoundCloud · A proposal · November 2026

A year-round program that carries artists past the first spike, on SoundCloud.

For Kalesha Madlani, SoundCloud From HUE Unlimited Stage An early idea Next step A call to workshop it

Built inside streaming. Ready for SoundCloud.

For the last four to five years, HUE has worked as an embedded partner with the leading streaming platform. We used that work to build the creative operations to run music programming and music initiatives at scale.

That’s why we’d be a good partner for a company like SoundCloud, and a genuinely interested one. We’d love to take those learnings and apply them to working with you.

SoundCloud has put out a call for music blogs, creators, pages, writers and streamers who want to work with it.11 A group that varied only works with an operations layer behind it. That’s what HUE’s creative operations do: brief each contributor, get everything out on time, and publish it in interesting ways. So we’d come in as a service partner, running that layer alongside your team, month after month.

01

SoundCloud has changed how artists get paid. The story still lives in one-off moments.

In twelve months SoundCloud cut its distribution fee to zero, started testing commission-free sales, and kept paying by the fan, with no minimum streams.123 Its listeners play new music nearly twice as much as the industry.4

The work that turns that into culture is still built one piece at a time: a month of breakout support, an episode of SoundCheck, a scene named in an annual report.564 You’ve brought Nina Protocol’s editorial archive in to surface emerging artists and scenes, and you’re building a network of creator ambassadors.78 The parts are there. What we don’t see yet is the thing that carries an artist from a first spike to a fifth release.

  • 0%distribution fee since November 20251
  • 100%of royalties to the artist, on and off SoundCloud1
  • ~2×new-music listening against the industry4
  • 40M+artists on the platform9

You’ve rebuilt the money side. Now the story needs a rhythm.

02

A first sketch, in four pieces. Rough on purpose.

Starting points, not a spec. Each one is as much a question for your team as an idea.

  1. 01
    Sketch

    Frequency × SoundCloud: the scene issue

    Editorial on a scene and the artists inside it, with every track playable on SoundCloud. Which scenes, and how often, is open.

  2. 02
    Sketch

    HUE FM stations

    Human-picked SoundCloud playlists that travel with the editorial. Where they live is a question for you.

  3. 03
    Sketch

    Sessions in real rooms

    Something live, made with local creators, that a brand partner could help fund.6 The format is ours to find together.

  4. 04
    Sketch

    A path to release on SoundCloud

    A plain way for the artists we feature to release and earn through SoundCloud for Artists.1310 Each artist decides for themselves.

Four starting points. The shape comes from the conversation.

03

How it could start. Small, then whatever earns it.

We’d rather not guess at phases and dates before we’ve talked.

  1. Talk

    A call to see whether there’s something here, and what it would need to be.

  2. Try

    A small first run, sized together around what your team cares about.

  3. Decide

    Keep what works, drop what doesn’t, and only then talk about what it grows into.

Start small. Let the results decide the rest.

04

What success could look like. To be defined with you.

A few places we’d look first. The real measures come from what matters to your team.

  • DiscoveryDo featured artists find new listeners on SoundCloud?
  • ArtistsDo they start, and keep, releasing and earning there?
  • ContributorsDo blogs, creators, pages, writers and streamers keep showing up for it?
  • MemoryDoes it leave a record of scenes and artists worth pointing back to?

Measured on SoundCloud. Set together.

06

Let’s workshop it. The rest gets built on the call.

The useful part is the conversation.

What we’d want to learn from you

  1. 01Where you’re headed

    What artist and creator partnerships need to do for SoundCloud next year.

  2. 02Who matters most

    The scenes, artists and contributors you most want to reach.

  3. 03What’s already moving

    Programs and partners already running, so this fits around them, not over them.

  4. 04Whether it’s worth building

    And if it is, what a first version would need to show.

What this is

  • An early idea, and an invitation to workshop it with your team.
  • One conversation first. Anything after that, we’d shape together.
  • HUE’s creative operations, and outlets like Frequency and HUE FM, pointed at SoundCloud’s artists and tools.

What it isn’t

  • Not a quote. Scope and support come after we’ve talked.
  • Not a claim that any artist is attached. Every artist chooses for themselves.
  • Not a finished plan. Every piece here is a sketch.

Kalesha, would you be up for finding time to workshop this with us? It’s an early sketch on purpose. If it’s interesting to you, discovery on that call would help us bake out the rest together.
