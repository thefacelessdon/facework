The Long Run.

HUE × SoundCloud · A proposal · November 2026

A year-round program that carries artists past the first spike, on SoundCloud.

For SoundCloud Artist Partnerships From HUE Unlimited Format Editorial, stations, sessions, a cohort Next step One working session

Built inside streaming. Ready for SoundCloud.

For the last four to five years, HUE has worked as an embedded partner with the leading streaming platform. We used that work to build the creative operations to run music programming and music initiatives at scale.

That’s why we’d be a good partner for a company like SoundCloud, and a genuinely interested one. We’d love to take those learnings and apply them to working with you: a partner that runs the program alongside your team, month after month.

01

SoundCloud already pays artists like no one else. The story still lives in one-off moments.

In twelve months SoundCloud cut its distribution fee to zero, opened commission-free sales, and kept paying by the fan, with no minimum streams.123 Its listeners play new music nearly twice as much as the industry.4

The work that turns that into culture is still built one piece at a time: a month of breakout support, an episode of SoundCheck, a scene named in a year-end report.564 You’ve brought Nina Protocol’s editorial archive in to surface emerging artists and scenes, and you’re building a network of creator ambassadors.78 The parts are there. What’s missing is the thing that carries an artist from a first spike to a fifth release.

  • 0%distribution fee since November 20251
  • 100%of royalties to the artist, on and off SoundCloud1
  • ~2×new-music listening against the industry4
  • 40M+artists on the platform9

You’ve rebuilt the money side. Now the story needs a rhythm.

02

Four pieces, one rhythm. Every month, a scene is found, written, played and released.

One program, run month by month, with SoundCloud’s artist tools at the center. Every piece is a proposal, to shape with your team.

  1. 01
    Proposed

    Frequency × SoundCloud: the scene issue

    Each month, an editorial feature on one scene and the artists inside it, written and photographed the way Frequency is made, with every track playable on SoundCloud.

  2. 02
    Proposed

    HUE FM stations

    Ordered, human-picked SoundCloud playlists that travel with each issue. An hour you play front to back, picked by people, not a feed.

  3. 03
    Proposed

    The session, in a real room

    One session a month: soundcheck content made with local creators, the set, and a month of cutdowns after. Built on formats SoundCloud already makes with brands, so a partner can fund an edition.6

  4. 04
    Proposed

    The cohort: release on SoundCloud

    Featured artists get a plain path to distribute, split and earn through SoundCloud for Artists: 100% of royalties, Split Pay, Fan Support and direct sales.1310 Each artist decides; we make it easy.

Found, written, played, released. Then again next month.

04

Measured on SoundCloud. Reported monthly, in plain numbers.

Informed by data, not driven by it. The targets are set with you in the working session.

  • DiscoveryPlays, likes, reposts and follows on featured artists, before and after their month.
  • ArtistsFeatured artists who start distributing or monetizing through SoundCloud for Artists, and who keep releasing.
  • FansFan Support and direct sales on featured releases, where artists turn them on.
  • ReachViews and engagement on editorial, sessions and cutdowns across artists’ and creators’ own channels.
  • MemoryA growing, linked record of scenes and artists that SoundCloud can keep pointing back to.

Five numbers, every month. The case for a second season writes itself.
