Images tagged “Imagined”

  • Made for Delta Futurism with image generation, in a present-day documentary style. They show ideas, not approved plans, and the people in them are not real.
  • The three imagined frames in The Present (a musician, a teacher, a business opening) stand in until the real people are photographed, with their permission.
  • The rule for replacing them: a real photograph of the same idea, labelled “Already here” with a real caption (source/shot-list.md).
