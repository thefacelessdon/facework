Built to Stay · A proposal to the Arkansas APEX Accelerator

Ready for Every Buyer.

Entergy’s two new plants in Jefferson County, Arkansas Cypress and Jefferson Power Station, bring county firms to the door. One readiness record can make them ready for Entergy’s contractors and for government buyers at the same time.

For: Kenneth Griffin Jr., Procurement Counselor, and Melanie Berman, Program DirectorDiscussion draft · November 2026Read Built to Stay →

What this is

  • An invitation to bring APEX’s free counseling to the Pine Bluff vendor workshops Built to Stay is organizing around the two builds.
  • Built on what APEX already started in Pine Bluff with the September 2026 contracting cohort.
  • Scoped to APEX’s work: registrations, certifications, capability statements, cybersecurity and bid matching for government buyers.

What it isn’t

  • Not a request to vouch for firms, endorse anyone, or match firms to private Entergy or Wanzek packages. Delta Futurism does that part.
  • Not a cost to APEX beyond counselor time. Workshop food and venue are covered by the hosts and Built to Stay.
  • Not a way in for our own business. The family’s restaurant may take part like any county firm, and the family steps out of any decision about it.

Contact: Stedmon Harper, Delta Futurism · harper@face.works

Why APEX,
and Why Now.

What We’d Do
Together.

One workshop format, run in Pine Bluff, with each partner doing only what it already does.

PieceAPEXHostsDelta Futurism
1Get registered

A module on SAM and UEI, the state minority- and women-owned business certification, and SBA certifications.

The Pine Bluff Black Chamber, the UAPB Business Support Incubator and The Generator host the room.

Office hours on workshop days; intake for each firm that wants it; a capability statement and a cybersecurity check.

Space for one-on-ones.

Keeps each firm’s readiness record, citing APEX counseling only with the firm’s consent.

3Find the work

Daily bid matching for public work near Jefferson County.

—

Matches ready firms to Wanzek’s packages on the private build, and sends notice the day one opens.

4Count it

Records what it already reports: new government-ready firms, certifications, referrals, awards.

—

A monthly count of firms registered, prequalified, bidding and awarded on the build.

Most of what makes a firm ready is the same for every buyer: registrations, insurance, a safety program, a capability statement, clean books. Built to Stay keeps one record per firm, so the work done for one buyer counts for the next.
