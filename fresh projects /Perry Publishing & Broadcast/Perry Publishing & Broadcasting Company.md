***How We Got Started***  
In 1979, Russell M. Perry started the Perry Publishing Company with it's first edition Oklahoma City's *The Black Chronicle* newspaper.  
*The Black Chronicle* newspaper, a weekly periodical servicing the Oklahoma City metro area's African American population, has grown into one of the largest paid weekly newspapers in the state of Oklahoma.  Currently, *The Black Chronicle* services the entire state of Oklahoma \- (i.e., Tulsa, Lawton/Ft. Sill, and Oklahoma City).

**Continuous Growth**  
In 1993, with the purchase of KVSP 1140 AM in Oklahoma City, Perry formed the Perry Publishing & Broadcasting Company, Inc. KVSP AM became the first urban-formatted radio station for Oklahoma City in more than fifteen years.

 In 2003, PPBC built a 2,000 ft tower in Caddo County, Oklahoma to move KRPT FM into Oklahoma City making it a Full C in power, a 100,000 watts. KRPT FM call letters were changed to KVSP FM and Oklahoma City had its first Urban FM radio station in more than 15 years.

***Company history***

Perry Publishing and Broadcasting was founded in April 1979 by [Russell M. Perry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_M._Perry) and began publishing the [*Black Chronicle*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Chronicle), Oklahoma's oldest African-American community newspaper, that same month.[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perry_Publishing_and_Broadcasting#cite_note-5)

In 1993, Perry Publishing acquired its first radio station with the purchase of AM 1140 (now known as [KRMP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KRMP)) in Oklahoma City.[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perry_Publishing_and_Broadcasting#cite_note-:0-6) Then acquired [KVSP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KVSP) and became the first [urban contemporary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urban_contemporary) station in the Oklahoma City market since the early-1990s. With the success of KVSP, Perry soon acquired [KJMM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KJMM) in [Tulsa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulsa,_Oklahoma) in 1994\.[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perry_Publishing_and_Broadcasting#cite_note-:0-6)

In 1996, Perry Publishing acquired its first duopoly with [KJMZ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KJMZ) and [KKRX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KKRX), serving the [Lawton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawton,_Oklahoma) area.[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perry_Publishing_and_Broadcasting#cite_note-:0-6)

In 1999, Perry Publishing purchased [KGTO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KGTO) in Tulsa from [Cox Radio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cox_Radio), providing the company a duopoly in Tulsa along with KJMM.[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perry_Publishing_and_Broadcasting#cite_note-:0-6)

In 2001, Perry Publishing acquired five more stations serving the Lawton area: [KKEN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KKEN), [KDDQ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KDDQ), [KRPT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KVSP), [KPNS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KFTP), and [KXCA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KXCA).[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perry_Publishing_and_Broadcasting#cite_note-:0-6)

In 2005, Perry then acquired [KACO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KACO_\(FM\)), which also serving the Lawton area.[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perry_Publishing_and_Broadcasting#cite_note-:0-6)

In August 2007, Perry Publishing acquired [Radio One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radio_One_\(company\))'s cluster of stations in [Augusta, Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augusta,_Georgia): [WFXA-FM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WFXA-FM), [WAKB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WAKB), [WTHB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WTHB), and [WAEG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WAEG).[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perry_Publishing_and_Broadcasting#cite_note-7)

In November 2015, it was announced that Perry Publishing purchased [KFMD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KQIS_\(AM\)) in [Bethel Heights, Arkansas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bethel_Heights,_Arkansas) from Hog Radio for $400,000.[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perry_Publishing_and_Broadcasting#cite_note-8)[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perry_Publishing_and_Broadcasting#cite_note-9)

In December 2018, Perry Publishing announced that it would sell all 8 of his station in the Lawton area to Mollman Media for $3 million.[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perry_Publishing_and_Broadcasting#cite_note-10) The sale was completed in April 2019\.[\[11\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perry_Publishing_and_Broadcasting#cite_note-11)
