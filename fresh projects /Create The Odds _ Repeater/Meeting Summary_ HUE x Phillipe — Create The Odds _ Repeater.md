Meeting Summary: HUE x Phillipe — Create The Odds / Repeater  
*April 30, 2026*

---

**Who is Phillipe?**

Phillipe runs Create The Odds, a new music supervision company (currently a solo operation). His background is in sync licensing — before launching Create The Odds, he notably worked at Raedio, and prior to that at a trailer music licensing company where he built to $300K–$400K/year in sales, eventually exceeding $1M before leaving. That earlier role is also where he first connected with Anthony, the Italian composer who is central to this opportunity.

---

**The Opportunity: Two Entities, One Partnership**

Phillipe is formalizing a partnership between two companies that function as "brother and sister":

*Create The Odds* — The agency / client-facing side

* Music supervision for TV/film (current clients include *Real Housewives of Atlanta*, *Mortal Kombat*, and work with Ludwig for Ryan Coogler)  
* Phillipe will centralize all music supervision work here going forward  
* Acts as the brand storytelling and advocacy layer — the name reflects the mission: most creators face long odds, this company creates the opportunities to beat them  
* Will serve as the agency arm for Repeater

*Repeater* — The creative / production side

* Led by Anthony and his partner Jonny  
* Strong musical output, no client-facing presence  
* Jonny handles business ops; not a front-facing personality  
* Phillipe steps in as sales  
* Website: [thisisrepeater.com](https://www.thisisrepeater.com/)

The business model focus is custom music — primarily for trailers and advertising, which commands $30K–$65K per custom, versus $2K–$5K for standard TV/film syncs. The plan is to build out a library of these customs while Phillipe drives the client relationships.

---

**Adjacent Relationship: AudioVybez**

[AudioVybez](https://www.audiovybez.com/) is another company in Phillipe's orbit worth tracking. Phillipe has been working with AudioVybez and through that relationship, opportunities to deliver custom songs for video games and TV have become apparent. Their exact positioning within the broader structure is still being defined, but they represent a meaningful node in his network and a potential channel for where work — and revenue — can flow.

---

**The Ask**

They're looking for branding and storytelling strategy for both entities — treated individually but cohesively. Phillipe referenced [Baxter House Music](https://www.baxterhousemusic.com/) as a directional example: a company that punches above its musical weight because of strong branding and presentation. The goal is to give both Create The Odds and Repeater that kind of authoritative visual and narrative presence.

Deliverables they have in mind:

* Website for each  
* Social presence  
* Brand bible with guidelines
