## 5. General Grant of Authority

I grant my agent full authority to exercise or perform on my behalf any act, power, duty, right, or obligation relating to any property, real or personal, tangible or intangible, now owned or hereafter acquired by me, including the specifically enumerated powers below. My agent has full power and authority to do everything necessary to exercise these powers as fully as I could if personally present, with full power of substitution or revocation, and I ratify all that my agent shall lawfully do under this instrument.

The enumeration of specific powers below is **not** intended to limit or restrict the general authority granted in this Section.

### A. Collection and Payment
To forgive, request, demand, sue for, recover, collect, receive, and hold all sums of money, debts, dues, commercial paper, checks, drafts, accounts, deposits, annuities, pensions, profit-sharing and retirement benefits, Social Security, insurance and other contractual benefits and proceeds, and all documents of title and property rights; and to adjust, sell, compromise, and to execute and deliver releases, receipts, and discharges for the same.

### B. Acquisition and Sale
To acquire, purchase, exchange, grant options to sell, and convey real or personal property, tangible or intangible, or interests therein, on such terms as my agent deems proper.

### C. Management of Property
To maintain, repair, improve, manage, insure, rent, lease, encumber, and in any manner deal with any real or personal property, or any interest therein, that I now own or may hereafter acquire.

### D. Banking
To make, receive, and endorse checks and drafts; deposit and withdraw funds; acquire and redeem certificates of deposit; and execute such deeds of trust, leases, or other security arrangements as may be necessary. My agent is specifically authorized to sign all checks in my name, make bank deposits and withdrawals, and transact all banking business on my behalf.

### E. Motor Vehicles
To apply for a Certificate of Title to, and to endorse and transfer title to, any motor vehicle, and to represent in any transfer that title is free of liens except as specifically set forth.

### F. Business Interests
To conduct or participate in any lawful business; to execute partnership agreements and amendments; to incorporate, reorganize, merge, consolidate, recapitalize, sell, liquidate, or dissolve any business; to elect or employ officers, directors, and agents; to carry out agreements for the sale of any business interest; and to exercise stock options. **This authority expressly includes the operation of, and dealing with the membership or ownership interest in, any limited liability company or other entity that holds family assets (including the restaurant business).**

### G. Insurance and Annuities
To engage in insurance and annuity transactions, including applying for, maintaining, canceling, paying premiums on, increasing or decreasing coverage of, collecting on, borrowing against, transferring ownership of, surrendering, or purchasing insurance policies and annuities. **This authority expressly includes my Prudential Variable Appreciable Life policy (#98 727 915)** — including paying premiums to keep it in force, managing or repaying any policy loan, accessing the Living Needs Benefit or other riders, and updating the beneficiary designation to name a trust (subject to Section 8 below).

### H. Taxes
To prepare, sign, and file joint or separate income, gift, and estate tax returns and declarations; to consent to gifts and utilize gift-splitting and other tax elections; to represent me before the IRS and state, local, and foreign tax authorities (including signing IRS Form 2848 or 8821); to receive confidential tax information; to receive and deposit refunds; and to execute waivers, consents, offers in compromise, and closing agreements.

### I. Safe-Deposit Boxes
To have access to any safe-deposit box rented by me, to remove all or part of the contents, and to surrender any such box.

### J. Government Benefits
To deal with the Social Security Administration, Veterans Administration, Medicare, Medicaid, and all other government benefit and entitlement programs, including applications, planning for eligibility, appeals, and serving as representative payee.

### K. Claims and Litigation
To assert and prosecute claims, defend and settle litigation, and submit matters to arbitration, in any proceeding relating to my property or interests.

## 6. Trust Powers (Specifically Granted)

I **specifically grant** my agent the authority to **create, establish, and fund** one or more inter vivos trusts of any type (whether revocable or irrevocable) for my benefit and consistent with my estate plan, and to transfer any of my real or personal property into any such trust. With respect to any trust created by me or on my behalf, my agent may **add property to, amend, modify, revoke, or terminate** the trust, and may accept transfers or distributions from any trustee of any trust in which I have an interest, whether as grantor, beneficiary, or otherwise.

Without limiting the foregoing, my agent may:

(i) Create and fund a sole-benefit trust under 42 U.S.C. § 1396p(c)(2)(B);
(ii) Create and fund a self-settled special-needs trust under 42 U.S.C. § 1396p(d)(4)(A);
(iii) Create and fund a qualified income trust under 42 U.S.C. § 1396p(d)(4)(B) if needed to qualify me for Medicaid; and
(iv) Execute documents to join and transfer assets to a pooled trust under 42 U.S.C. § 1396p(d)(4)(C).

> **Purpose note (not part of the legal grant):** This power is the mechanism that lets my agent place the family land, the solar-lease interest, the restaurant entity, the Prudential life-insurance proceeds, and other assets into the Family Operating Trust if I am unable to do so myself.

## 7. Gifting Powers (Specifically Granted)

I **specifically grant** my agent the authority to **make gifts** of any interest I have in real or personal property, outright or in trust, including gifts that exceed the annual federal gift-tax exclusion under IRC § 2503(b), and to consent to gift-splitting under IRC § 2513, to or for the benefit of those persons or entities (which may include my agent and my descendants) to whom such property would pass under my estate plan. All gifts shall be made consistent with: (1) the resources needed for my care; (2) the goal of preserving the largest possible estate for my beneficiaries; (3) my pattern of prior giving; and (4) my existing estate-planning documents. I authorize my agent to engage in estate, financial, Medicaid, long-term-care, and asset-preservation planning, in consultation with an attorney experienced in such matters.

---

## 8. Beneficiary and Survivorship Powers (Specifically Granted)

I **specifically grant** my agent the authority to **create or change beneficiary designations** and **rights of survivorship** on my property — including bank and investment accounts, insurance policies and annuities, retirement plans, and real property interests — and to execute beneficiary deeds (including an enhanced life-estate "lady bird" deed). This authority expressly includes naming a trust as the beneficiary of my Prudential life-insurance policy.
