## Design Context

### Users

Facework serves practitioners, architects, implementers, runtime developers,
maintainers, collaborators, and prospective clients encountering an emerging
discipline and standards ecosystem.

They need to understand how Facework thinks, inspect its theory and practice,
evaluate legitimacy through evidence, and inherit artifacts without depending on
the original creator or tool. The public experience should permit intuitive entry
through Field Notes, Models, Frameworks, Cases, Conversations, Experiments,
Library, and About while preserving canonical lineage beneath the surface.

### Brand Personality

Facework is **calm, exacting, and alive**.

It should communicate confidence, authority, continuity, structural clarity, and
human intelligence. It is not a personality-led consultancy, a generic creative
agency, a startup protocol microsite, or a trend-sensitive AI product.

### Aesthetic Direction

The visual system combines two physical references:

1. a durable public standard or technical field manual;
2. a strategist's notebook whose observations, revisions, and lineage remain
   visible.

The result should feel editorial-institutional, engineered without sterility,
minimal with meaning, and capable of supporting dense evidence as well as quiet
reflection. Paper and field modes are coordinated expressions of the same system.

Anti-references include generic SaaS dashboards, luxury-minimal agency sites,
crypto-neon futurism, lifestyle transformation brands, decorative butterfly
identities, and technical typography used as costume.

### Design Principles

1. **Meaning precedes expression.** Every visible decision must have a structural
   or communicative purpose.
2. **Architecture remains discoverable.** Dependency, lineage, status, and
   evidence are revealed progressively rather than hidden or dumped on the user.
3. **Authority without ornament.** Rigor and permanence come from exact
   relationships, not institutional theater.
4. **Transmission over performance.** Artifacts remain clear, durable,
   accessible, and inheritable across tools and generations.
5. **Calm is controlled energy.** Facework is neither loud nor inert; tension,
   rhythm, and motion remain purposeful.
6. **Identity lives in behavior.** The mark, typography, color, and composition
   express the system but do not replace it.
