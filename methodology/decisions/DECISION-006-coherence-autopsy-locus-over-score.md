# FW-DEC-006 — Locus over score

## Question

Horizon entry **H1** claimed the governing equation
`Coherence = (Flow × Resonance) / (1 + Entropy)` explains failures retroactively
from public record alone. Two runs tested it against nine external subjects
(`methodology/h1-coherence-autopsies-2026-08-19.md`,
`…-run2-2026-08-19.md`), plus two subjects scored for this record.

The runs returned a split verdict. **The scalar score did not do what the
tracker implies it does. The locus assignment did, ten times out of ten.** That
split has to be ruled on, because every scored artifact in this repo — the
tracker, `COMPLIANCE.md`, `CERTIFICATION.md` — currently presents a **number**
as the finding.

Three dispositions were available: keep the scalar as the headline and treat
locus as commentary; demote the scalar and make locus the finding; or hold H1 in
the Horizon pending more subjects.

## Decision

**The locus is the finding. The scalar is a severity summary and must never be
reported alone.** H1 is promoted out of the Horizon as method, on its revised
claim — not its original one.

Seven rulings:

1. **H1 is promoted** on the revised claim: *the equation localises failure to a
   protocol layer, retroactively, from public record alone.* The original claim —
   that the equation "explains failures" via its score — is **not** promoted.
2. **A diagnostic MUST emit a locus and a failing term, not only a zone.** A
   coherence report consisting of a number and a colour is incomplete.
3. **The scalar MUST NOT be reported bare at Resonance ≥ 4** (finding F7 below).
4. **Coherence autopsy is adopted as a methodology-layer method** with three
   binding requirements: the locus is named *before* insider accounts are
   consulted; every batch carries at least one survivor control, matched to a
   subject where a structural twin exists; every load-bearing figure is verified
   against a source, and unverifiable claims are **dropped, not hedged**.
5. **`CERTIFICATION.md` must state that it grades protocol discipline and makes
   no survivability claim** (finding F4). The five-dimension 100-point total is
   *not* restructured — see Alternatives rejected.
6. **Locus base rates are recorded and MUST accompany any locus call** (F9). A
   call in a high-base-rate layer carries less information than one in a rare
   layer, and a measurement discipline that hides its priors is selling
   confidence it has not earned.
7. **Two failure archetypes are recorded as canon-adjacent patterns** and handed
   to H2 as its first testable objects.

### A7 — Ofo (China, 2016 – collapse from Dec 2018)

Flow **2**, Resonance **3**, SI **1**, Entropy **5** → **(2 × 3) / 6 = 1.0**.

Deposits of ¥99, later ¥199, were taken from users before rental. By late 2018
**over 12 million users had queued for refunds**, rising to **~15 million by
2020**; refunding all users required roughly **¥1.2bn**, against a company that
had raised **more than $2bn** and was already missing supplier payments. Refund
throughput fell from ~20,000 users/day in late 2018 to an estimated **~10/day**
by 2020.

**Locus, named before the record was read: Frequency** — customer deposits were
treated as working capital, so a diffuse obligation existed with no funding model
behind it. **Secondary: Field** — adoption was bought with subsidy rather than
earned as a norm, which is why Resonance never converted into a defensible
position. **Confirmed.** The failure is described in the record as a cash-flow
crisis *caused in part by refunds and supplier payments* — i.e. the obligation,
not the demand.

### C3 — Tecnobrega, Belém (matched control, non-Anglophone)

Flow **5**, Resonance **4**, SI **4**, Entropy **2** → **(5 × 4) / 3 = 6.67**.

Scored as an **out-of-sample test of F6**, the thesis derived from run 2's matched
pair: *a scene survives commercial contact only if it owns the circuit its energy
travels through.* Prediction before checking: DEEP GREEN.

Tecnobrega inverts the usual asset. Recordings are **born free** — copyright is
not part of the model, and CDs circulate as marketing for the weekly
*aparelhagem* sound-system parties, where artists are actually paid. The scene
owns production, distribution and venue economics end to end, was releasing on the
order of **400 records a year** against roughly 30 from the majors in Brazil, and
Belém alone turned over **several million USD annually**. Primary study:
**Lemos & Castro, *Tecnobrega: O Pará reinventando o negócio da música* (2008)**,
from 2006 field research in the Belém metropolitan region.

**F6 confirmed out-of-sample**, in a different hemisphere, language and economy,
by the same mechanism as Hospital Records: *own the circuit.*

## The non-Anglophone gap — closed, with a precise caveat

Run 2 recorded that all subjects were Global North and every source Anglophone,
and made that blocking. A7 (China) and C3 (Brazil) close it: **nine subjects,
five countries, four continents.**

The honest caveat: A7's record was read through the **English editions** of
Chinese outlets (Caixin Global, SCMP, China Daily) rather than Chinese-language
originals. C3's primary source is genuinely Portuguese-language and was identified
as such. So the gap is closed for *cultural and economic range* and **partially**
closed for *linguistic independence of sources*. That residue is not blocking; it
is recorded as a known limit of the evidence base.

## F9 — Locus base rates, and why they must be published

Across seven dead subjects:

| Primary locus | Layer | Count | Subjects |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Frequency** | 4 | **3 / 7** | Vine, BHS, Ofo |
| Semantics | 1 | 1 / 7 | Quibi |
| Field | 2 | 1 / 7 | grunge |
| Stability | 5 | 1 / 7 | Friendster |
| Consonance | 7 | 1 / 7 | Nokia |

Frequency is the **modal** locus at 43%. A Frequency call is therefore weak
evidence — it is close to the prior — while a Consonance or Field call is strong,
because it departs from it. Publishing the prior is what separates a measurement
discipline from a reading. **Ruling 6 makes it mandatory.**

The Frequency cluster then **decomposes into two archetypes**, which restores its
information value:

- **The extractive parent** — a subsidiary whose survival is subordinate to its
  parent's interests, with no boundary preventing extraction. *Vine* (Twitter
  refused creator economics to protect portfolio precedent), *BHS* (reserves
  extracted to the owner).
- **The unfunded promise** — a diffuse obligation to many holders, treated as
  working capital. *Ofo* (user deposits), *BHS* (the £571m pension deficit).

BHS sits in both, which is consistent with it being the fastest total collapse of
an 88-year-old system in the set. **Both archetypes are detectable in a living
system**, which is precisely what H2 needs and did not have.

## Alternatives rejected

**Restructure `COMPLIANCE.md` into two totals** (survivability vs quality) to fix
F4. Rejected as disproportionate: it invalidates the rubric's version history and
every scored artifact for a defect that one honest sentence in `CERTIFICATION.md`
resolves, and `CERTIFICATION.md` §1 already says *"a guarantee of protocol
discipline and artifact integrity"* — the fix is to stop the surrounding language
implying more.

**Keep the scalar as the headline.** Rejected on the evidence: A4 (grunge) died
and scores AMBER, "holds but fragile." A single number cannot separate a
dissolved system from a fragile living one.

**Hold H1 pending more subjects.** Rejected. Nine subjects, five countries, four
domains, three controls and ten held locus calls is more evidence than any
criterion on the 0.1.0–1.0.0 ladder currently rests on. The Horizon's own rule is
that an entry leaves by passing its test and earning a record. It passed.

**Add a truthfulness term for the Theranos class** (F3). Rejected as out of
scope; fitting an instrument to a case it was not built for. Recorded as a stated
boundary instead: coherence measures whether a system holds together, not whether
what it holds together around is real.

Not owed: any change to the equation, the zone table, or the five compliance
dimensions. Nothing in two runs contradicted the equation's **form** — A2 is
positive evidence for it, since a product numerator correctly reads
Resonance 5 / Flow 1 as incoherent where an additive one would not.

## Revisit trigger
