**Date:** 2026-08-19 · **Release earned:** 0.0.70 · **Track:** agency-studio ·
**Evidence:** signaled · **Artifacts produced:** 41 · **Phases:** 8 of 8, all gated

**Coherence 4.00 GREEN** · failing term **Flow** · primary locus **Consonance**
(1/7 in the base rates → **strong**, departing from the 3/7 Frequency mode) ·

The project itself is a client engagement and is excluded from this repo. This retro
carries only what the **practice** learned.

## The headline

**A coherence pass introduced an economic parameter as a side effect of resolving a
document conflict, and nothing downstream was watching for it.**

Two source documents disagreed on a programme's duration. `/fw-consonance` reconciled
them with the phrase *"eighteen months is the programme arc across ~3 cohorts."* That
phrase was chosen for its reconciling power. It became the intake cadence, which sized
the largest cost line and capped the conversion rate at a third of the alternative —
and it appeared in two artifacts **2× apart**, because nothing owned it.

**Consonance had no obligation to log it, because the protocol treated Consonance as a
checker rather than a decider. It is both, and only one was gated.**

## Three further additions

- **PROTOCOL §6.1 — four pre-gate checks.** Version currency · citation resolution ·
  count agreement · **mechanism diff**. Checks 1–3 verify claims tracing to a locked
  decision. **Check 4 exists because they share that blind spot:** they cannot catch two
  phases independently designing one mechanism two incompatible ways, since neither
  contradicts anything on the record. In this run 1–3 found three defects and **4 found
  the worst one.** *Agreement about what was decided is not agreement about what happens.*
- **Entropy Step 2.5 — capacity denominators.** Compute `capacity / throughput` before
  auditing selection criteria. Four phases had treated a readiness bar as load-bearing;
  the denominator showed the bar was never the binding constraint.
- **Sovereignty Step 2.5 — succession of obligations.** Instruments survive a departure;
  **practices do not, unless a successor is named.** Custodian is a role, never a person,
  never the role that changes hands. A handover completes on **confirmed receipt.**

## What worked, ranked by transferability

1. **Source all vocabulary from the primary source, never from the methodology.** Posture
   audit across 39 artifacts: zero violations.
2. **Canonical-source conventions** — one table for figures, one section per spec. Where
   followed, nothing drifted. Where a phase restated instead of citing, **three
   incompatible versions of the same artifact appeared.**
3. **Self-correction in place, wrong version left visible.** Two phases did it
   independently and reached the same correction from different directions.
4. **Phases writing hostile cold reads of their own output.** Four of six objections were
   later confirmed by independent phases.
5. **The pre-gate checks.** Cheap, mechanical, and check 4 caught a defect in the pass
   that invented it.

## What did not work

1. **Three phases run concurrently.** Each self-audited honestly, each passed its own
   gate, and two built the load-bearing deliverable incompatibly. Bought time, cost a full
   reconciliation pass.
2. **Machine manifests treated as prose.** They read as authoritative and are consumed by
   runtimes that cannot notice a dangling path.
3. **Phase weighting fixed at Phase 1** against evidence that arrived at Phase 6.
4. **No home for a parameter nobody decided.** Now §Step 4.5.

## Two honest caveats about this run

**It was non-interactive throughout, so `AskUserQuestion` was never available.** Every
decision was forced-and-reasoned rather than elicited. The protocol assumes a dialogue
that did not happen. That likely made the run faster and its self-corrections more
frequent — nobody was there to stop a phase disagreeing with itself — and it means the
diagnostic scores are one operator's assessment of his own work.

**The run cannot earn a minor bump, and did not claim one.** `0.1.0` means Independent
Validation — operation or review by someone who is not the author. This run was authored
and audited by the same operator. **It is a patch.** The author also mis-cited the
protocol version as `0.0.14` mid-run (that is the Sovereignty-loop guard-rail release,
not a version for the run) and corrected it on inspecting `VERSION` — which is a small
instance of the same class as gap 1: a value assumed rather than checked.
