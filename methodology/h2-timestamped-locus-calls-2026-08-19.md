## Pre-registration integrity rules

These bind, or the exercise is worthless:

1. **Scores and loci below are frozen.** They may not be edited after this
   commit's date. Corrections are **appended** with their own date, never
   substituted. The git history is the proof.
2. **Each call states its own falsification** before any outcome is known.
3. **Base-rate weight is declared per call** (FW-DEC-006 F9). A call in the modal
   layer is weak evidence and is labelled so; a call in a rare layer is strong.
4. **One "holds" call is included.** A method that only predicts doom is
   unfalsifiable in the direction that matters, so at least one subject per round
   is predicted *not* to break.
5. **The scalar is not the prediction.** Per FW-DEC-006 it may not be reported
   bare at Resonance ≥ 4; it appears here as severity context only.

**What H2 tests:** not *who* breaks — H1 proved the scalar cannot carry that —
but **where**. A call succeeds only if a break arrives in the named layer.

signature**, term for term: high Resonance, throttled Flow, and a parent making the
economics decision on portfolio logic rather than the subsidiary's survival.

close to the prior and should be discounted accordingly.

proximate mechanism is creator economics or parent-imposed constraint: a further
adverse change to splits or eligibility, a sale or wind-down, a mass creator
migration attributed to payout terms, or absorption into a parent product line.
**Resolves as WRONG-LAYER if** a material break occurs whose mechanism is
elsewhere — a moderation/legal rupture (Field), a platform-reliability collapse
(Stability), or an audience shift away from live streaming as a form (Resonance).

Severity context: **(4 × 5) / 4 = 5.0 — DEEP GREEN boundary.** By the scalar this
is the healthiest subject ever scored in this programme. **That is the point of
the call.**

**Locus call: Consonance (primary).**
The prediction is *not* commercial or technical failure. It is that the break, when
it comes, appears as a **divergence between declared governance and observed
control** — mission-control language and actual decision rights coming apart under
capital pressure, in a way that requires external adjudication rather than internal
correction.

**Base-rate weight: STRONG.** Consonance is rare (1/7) and departs sharply from
the prior. It is also the hardest call to make from the outside, since every layer
looks individually healthy — which is precisely the Nokia signature.

**Resolves as CORRECT if,** by 2028-08-19, a material governance rupture occurs
whose mechanism is the gap between stated mission structure and effective control:
regulatory or judicial intervention on the control question, a restructuring that
materially reduces Foundation control, or a public break among principals over
whether the declared structure is operative. **Resolves as WRONG-LAYER if** the
material break is a product, safety, competitive or capital-markets failure with no
governance-divergence mechanism. **Resolves as NO-BREAK if** the structure operates
unchallenged through the window.

*Note on standing:* this call is deliberately made on a subject where the analyst
has no access and no insider account. If locus calls only work with hindsight or
proximity, this is where that shows.

## What this round cannot establish

Three calls cannot produce a hit rate worth quoting. Round 1's job is to **start
the clock** and prove the pre-registration discipline holds — that scores, loci,
windows and falsifiers were fixed in public before outcomes were known, and that
the git record shows they were never touched. Subsequent rounds add subjects; the
first number worth citing needs an order of magnitude more calls than this.

**Known bias to watch:** two of three calls are US technology subjects, the same
narrowness H1 run 1 had. Round 2 should widen deliberately rather than accumulate
more of the easy cases.
